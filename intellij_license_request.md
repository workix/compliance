#### English
ABOUT US
BRIEF DESCRIPTION OF OUR PROJECT

AFTER ALL, WHAT IS WORKIX?
Workix is ​​a search engine; A project ; A website, A backend, a tool that aims to facilitate the tiring and tiring process of Candidate Selection and Job posting.

Workix's objective is to harmonize the Recruitment processes and reduce bad practices.

WHICH COMPANY ADMINISTERS WORKIX?
Workix is ​​not a company, there are no people behind the tool. Only the intelligence of the algorithm. People exist at the ends... who feed the system and take advantage of the information, reaching the final result, which is the hiring.

DIFFERENTIALS OF OUR TOOL.
Provide FeedBack
Who has never participated in a process and not even received an answer about what happened in the end?

Workix maintains the relationship of trust between the two ends, the process is transparent and communicable. Every Iteration is subject to alerts...

Accessible from Any Device
On Tablet, Cell Phone, Notebook, PC, Video Game... It doesn't matter the environment. Workix offers Responsive Website and Mobile Application. Same Information Everywhere.

REGISTER YOUR CURRICULUM (RESUME) AND VIEW WHERE YOU NEED IT...
Focus on Scalability and Growth
Workix aims for "Organic" Growth. New Tools can be implemented to increase the experience and automate processes.

**Validators and Verifiers can be used to ensure the Veracity of information exchanged by people.
Phantom or Nonexistent Vacancies in the Database
Workix has self-cleaning algorithms and ends the processes carried out with "Success" in the Database. Vacancies are valid. And everything that happens you know.

*Data Privacy
We do not store passwords or usernames. Only the Minimum Data to initiate the hiring process is stored.

You can also activate or deactivate the curriculum (resume) as needed. And you can request the removal of information if you wish.

** CPF for Individuals and CNPJ for Legal Entities, to maintain the integrity of the 1 x 1 Registry.

Obs: CPF is a single document for individuals, CNPJ is a single document for single document for legal entity

ANOTHER SEARCH TOOL THAT SELLS VIP ADS AND FEATURED SPOTS?
Another differential of Workix. We do not sell vacancies or access to content of dubious veracity. We provide the tool for people of common interest to meet. Employer x Employee.

GUARANTEES
We do not provide guarantees in the recruitment process.
We provide the search tool to find vacancies and give feedback. The selection process or any bad practice of its users is the responsibility of its authors.

AFINAL O QUE É WORKIX ?
Workix é uma ferramenta de busca ; Um projeto ; Um site , Um backend , uma ferramenta que visa facilitar o processo engessado e cansativo de Seleção de Candidatos e publicação de Empregos.

O objetivo da Workix é harmonizar os processos de Recrutamento e reduzir as más práticas.

QUAL EMPRESA ADMINISTRA A WORKIX?
Workix não é empresa, não há pessoas por trás da ferramenta. Somente a inteligência do algoritmo. Pessoas existem nas pontas ... que alimentam o sistema e usufruem das informações, chegando ao resultado final que é a contratação.

DIFERENCIAIS DA NOSSA FERRAMENTA.
Fornecer FeedBack
Quem nunca participou de um processo e nem ao menos recebeu uma resposta do que aconteceu no final?

Workix mantém a relação de confiança entre as duas pontas, o processo é transparente e comunicatível. Toda Iteração é passivel de alertas...

Ambiente Acessível de Qualquer dispositivo
No Tablet, No Celular, No Notebook, No Pc, No Vídeo Game... Não interessa o ambiente. Workix oferece Site Responsivo e Aplicativo Mobile. As Mesmas Informações em Todos os lugares.
